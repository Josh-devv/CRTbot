# The take profit is a fixed 3R, superseding the public 50 % / opposing-end targets

Every trade targets exactly three times its risk, and that target is never moved. This
supersedes the 50 % and opposing-end targets that appear in Sham's public material.

**Context:** The public sources recorded the take profit as *unverified* — variously 3R or
a 50 % / opposing-end exit — and the original research dossier carried that ambiguity
forward. The mentorship material resolves it unambiguously: the target is a fixed 3R, it is
never changed, and there is no scaling, no partials, no break-even move and no trailing.

**Consequences:** Trade management is fully mechanical — a trade runs to its 3R target or
its stop, nothing else. This is deliberately surprising to anyone reading only the public
material, so it is recorded here rather than left to be "corrected" later. The
`rMultiple` lives in configuration, but the *rule* that it is fixed is not configurable.
